## Assurance status at release

An independent hostile audit of this lineage returned **no release-blocking defect**.
Automated assurance passed — 13 of 13 verifier stages, 44 of 44 mutation fixtures
rejected, none skipped, no execution errors — and the final PDF passed a **human-reader
sign-off**, which no check in this repository can perform.

The documented boundaries of that assurance are not incidental and are restated here:
the published-PDF check binds word content in both directions but does **not** establish
semantic word order; the registered manuscript surface is a declared Markdown model
rather than the rendered page, with two measured differences recorded in
`visible_surface.py`; and the registry does not defend against a simultaneous edit of the
manuscript and the registry. The apparatus is not exhaustive and is not formally
verified. `V4_ASTRA_CORRECTION_LEDGER.md` Part 6 carries the full record.
